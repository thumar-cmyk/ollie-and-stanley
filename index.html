Ollie & Stanley

  • 🐾 Tap a cat to pick it, then tap the floor to walk. Each cat lives in its own room — tap a face button to go see that cat.
  • 🕷️ 🐭 Catch critters: walk onto spiders (+10) and mice (+25). A badge on a door means one is in that room.
  • 🐟 Food on the counter? Tap it to put it away (+3) before a cat steals it (−15).
  • 😾 Stanley chases Ollie! Escape (+5) or get caught (−10).
  • 🍪 Treat button, then tap the floor: the nearest cat runs to it (+2).
  • 💤 Leave a cat alone and it naps for points (double in a ☀️ sunny spot).
  • ✨ Tap sparkly things: sofas, benches, stairs and the cat tree (+5) to hop up; toy mice (+1); or be naughty with cups and the couch arm (−5).

Play with 2–3 phones

Pick who you are. One phone hosts and gets a code; the others type it in. Nobody picked? The cats play themselves.
